THE WATER MISTAKE

The Hidden Cost of Bad Timing

Vegetative ~Wks 1–4*

Early Pumping

Heavy early pumping or uniform weekly splits can deplete scarce borewells early.

Flowering ~Wks 5–10*

The Critical Squeeze

Borewell reserve drops right when maize enters tasseling, silking, and cob-forming.

Yield & Ripening ~Wks 11+*

The Impact

Incomplete cob fill and lighter grain weight at harvest.

!

Poorly timed irrigation can leave maize short of water during critical growth stages. KharifShield compares alternative ways to allocate a limited water budget.

*Week ranges are illustrative for a ~110-day hybrid; actual timing varies by hybrid, sowing date, and climate. 03 / 18

AGRONOMY BASIS

FAO-33 Maize Sensitivity Factors (Ky)

Whole-season Ky = 1.25 (separate seasonal reference)
Stage 1

Vegetative

Early leaf & root growth

Ky = 0.40

Lower sensitivity to mild water deficit.

★ PRIORITY Extreme

Flowering

Tasseling & Silking

Ky = 1.50

Highest yield penalty if water is short.

Stage 3

Yield Formation

Grain & cob filling

Ky = 0.50

Moderate sensitivity while grains fill.

Stage 4

Ripening

Maturation stage only

Ky = 0.20

Lowest deficit penalty near maturity.

Note: Ky measures relative yield sensitivity to ET deficit — it does not directly prescribe an irrigation percentage.

Source: FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 33, Table 12 (Whole-season Ky = 1.25 is a separate seasonal coefficient) 08 / 18

ACTUAL TECH STACK & AI ROLE

Prototype Architecture & Design Choices

Single-File Web App
(HTML / Tailwind / JS)

Runs in mobile or desktop browsers (requires CDN assets on first load) with no app-store install or backend server for the MVP.

Deterministic Engine

Water-split math is rule-based (guided by FAO-33 Ky weights), with Open-Meteo rain + sample weather fallback.

Where AI Is Used

Optional conversational Q&A helper (plain-language EN/తెలుగు explanations). Production deployment requires a backend proxy for API keys.

Allocation Math = Deterministic Rules (No ML) • Conversational AI = Farmer Q&A Only • ML Yield Prediction = Separate Planned Component.
