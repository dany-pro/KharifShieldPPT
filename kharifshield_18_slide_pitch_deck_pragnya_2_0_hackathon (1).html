THE WATER MISTAKE

The Hidden Cost of Bad Timing

Weeks 1–4 Early Pumping

Common Practice

Pump heavily when borewells are full, or split water evenly every week.

Weeks 5–10 Critical Stress

The Mid-Season Squeeze

Borewell reserve drops right when maize enters flowering and cob-forming.

Weeks 11+ Harvest Risk

The Impact

Incomplete cob fill and lighter grain weight at harvest.

!

Poorly timed irrigation can leave maize short of water during critical growth stages. KharifShield compares alternative ways to allocate a limited water budget.

AGRONOMY BASIS (CORRECTED)

FAO-33 Maize Sensitivity Factors (Ky)

Stage 1

Vegetative

Early leaf & root growth

Ky = 0.40

Lower sensitivity to mild water deficit.

★ PRIORITY Extreme

Flowering

Tasseling & Silking

Ky = 1.50

Highest yield penalty if water is short.

Stage 3

Yield Formation

Grain & cob filling

Ky = 0.50

Moderate sensitivity while grains fill.

Stage 4

Ripening

Maturation (Whole season: 1.25)

Ky = 0.20

Lowest deficit penalty near maturity.

Note: Ky is a yield-response sensitivity factor, not a fixed water percentage—it guides our heuristic to prioritize flowering/cob-forming weeks.
