THE WATER-TIMING TRAP

How Wrong Water Timing Hurts Maize Harvest

1. Young Plants ~Wks 1–4*

Early Waste

Pumping too much early—or every week—drains the borewell when small plants barely need it.

2. Cob-Forming ★ ~Wks 5–10*

Borewell Runs Dry

Water runs out right when tassels and cobs form—the exact weeks maize needs water most.

3. Ripening ~Wks 11+*

Smaller Harvest

Even if rain comes later, pollination loss is permanent—leaving half-filled cobs and lighter grain.

!

The same borewell water gives much better results when saved for Weeks 5–10 (Cob-Forming) instead of spent too early.

*Week ranges are illustrative for a ~110-day maize hybrid; actual timing varies by sowing date & climate. 04 / 18
